Research Background and Design Motivation

With the deep integration of digital technology and museum scenarios, innovative forms such as AR navigation and AI explanations are becoming important means to attract young people. However, existing solutions generally have problems such as homogenized experiences, insufficient interactivity, and poor lightweight adaptation: traditional AR mini-game development costs are high and performance pressure is large, while most navigation applications have single interaction forms and are difficult to truly stimulate user participation. This project aims to "bring cultural relics to life", targeting the pain points of young people's perception of museum experiences as dull and low participation, and designs a lightweight check-in navigation system based on Unity WebAR and WebGL. Through AR real-scene navigation, intelligent explanations, and check-in interactions, it enhances users' participation willingness and cultural perception without the need to download an APP.

3 Core Features

1. AR Check-in & Achievement System
Users scan exhibits with their phones to trigger AR check-ins, unlocking virtual badges, achievements, and hidden easter eggs. Solves the pain point of "lack of motivation to explore," transforming passive viewing into active exploration.
2. AI Smart Guide & Voice Interaction
After scanning, users receive personalized explanations generated by Gemini 1.5 Flash and synthesized by Azure TTS. They can also ask questions for easy-to-understand answers. Solves dull, one-way explanations with engaging dialogue.
3. Lightweight WebAR Navigation
No app download required. Open the webpage to start AR real-scene navigation, following virtual arrows to exhibits with check-in prompts. Solves indoor navigation difficulties and ensures low-threshold access.

Design & Technology Implementation

Visual Design Selection

We compared three visual directions: Chinese style, dark theme, and minimalist white.
- Chinese style: Rejected due to visual clutter and information overload when combined with AR content.
- Dark theme: Rejected due to poor AR detail visibility and low shareability in low-light environments.
- Selected: Minimalist white. High contrast makes AR exhibits and UI elements clear, works well in dim exhibition halls, and is ideal for photo sharing.

Technical Implementation

The system is built on lightweight WebAR. Users enter the webpage without downloading an app. The camera locks onto exhibits via image recognition, overlays 3D models from a local library, and runs AI dialogue and path guidance in parallel. Three data streams converge in the lightweight webpage to support a zero-threshold immersive experience through "navigation, identification, and dialogue."
