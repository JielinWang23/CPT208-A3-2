Research Background and Design Motivation

With the deep integration of digital technology and museum scenarios, innovative forms such as AR navigation and AI explanations are becoming important means to attract young people. However, existing solutions generally have problems such as homogenized experiences, insufficient interactivity, and poor lightweight adaptation: traditional AR mini-game development costs are high and performance pressure is large, while most navigation applications have single interaction forms and are difficult to truly stimulate user participation. This project aims to "bring cultural relics to life", targeting the pain points of young people's perception of museum experiences as dull and low participation, and designs a lightweight check-in navigation system based on UnityWebAR and WebGL. Through AR real-scene navigation, intelligent explanations, and check-in interactions, it enhances users' participation willingness and cultural perception without the need to download an APP.
